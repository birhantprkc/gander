PDFs have text in them now. You can select it, copy it, have a screen reader read it aloud, and search the whole document rather than the few pages on screen. Long files show which page you are on, take a scroll thumb you can drag, and let you jump straight to a page. Documents follow your dark mode, the home screen lays itself out for a tablet, an audio file gets its own screen instead of the video player, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean PDFs no longer lose text.
